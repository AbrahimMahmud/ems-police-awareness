# The record

What the confirmatory analysis was held to, and where each part lives. The paper cites these; the gate
(`scripts/23_regression_suite.py`, [[Gate and checks]]) holds the documents to their corrected wording.

- **`docs/CONFIRMATION_PLAN.md`** — the frozen pre-registration text (byte-identical above the addendum marker;
  `D.addendum_complete`) plus a numbered addendum §1–§30. Key sections: §9 estimator and the dose-response arm;
  §14 what was decided after discovery results were seen (the test window, the second primary outcome and the
  joint statistic are non-blind, rows 3, 14, 28 of the summary table); §15 the freeze incidents (see
  [[Freeze incidents]]); §18 the declared O2 coverage access; §19 rule 2, the reading of a non-rejection against
  the pre-freeze minimum detectable effect; §21 the within-block randomization scheme; §23 the reading rules as
  code (23.1c sensitivity survival, 23.1d pooled reading, 23.1e secondary arms, 23.11–23.12 the bound's
  limitations); §25 the fallback for an uncertified null (not invoked); §26 exploratory scripts pinned to the
  discovery window; §30 findings P27–P58 of the third specification audit. Summary table rows 1–31 with a
  blindness column (row 30 is the B-HEARD covariate).
- **`docs/PRE_ANALYSIS_NOTE.md`** — the hypotheses (H1 the test; H2 and H3 declared but not run, deviation 12),
  §4 the design table (discovery explored; confirmation entered no model or test before the lift except as
  F1–F5 record), §9.4 the interpretation table (row 5 is the pre-specified null), §10 the eight-test family,
  §11 the ledger of what is and is not pre-specified (§11.4 the incidents; §11.6 irreducible limitations).
- **`docs/PAPER_MASTER.md`** — the source document: §5.3 the five incidents and their disposition (the external
  pre-registration remedy foreclosed at the lift; proceeding was the author's decision of 2026-09-19), §6 the
  strata the code estimates, §7.4 the calibration certificates, §7.5b power, §8 discovery, §8b the sealed result
  (see [[Phase I]]), §9 limitations.
- **Registers.** `docs/AUDIT_FINDINGS.csv` — 164 findings, all fixed; id families P (specification audits, 63),
  X (infrastructure, 20), T (treatment, 17), N, D, E, S, L, O, F (incidents, 5), R, RI, B; dimensions
  design_logic 39, spec_vs_code 26, treatment 23, verification 14, estimator 13 … manuscript_vs_record 5 (CP3).
  `docs/CLAIMS_REGISTER.csv` — 1,354 claims (PAPER_MASTER 726, PAPER 627, SOURCE_REGISTER 1), 1,128 of them
  generated by `ops/phase_i_tables.py`. `data/reference/freeze_access_log.csv` — 15 rows of declared accesses.
- **Timestamps.** The pre-registration is internal: git commit timestamps, no external deposit; the paper says
  so (Limitation 9, RECORD 22.1).
